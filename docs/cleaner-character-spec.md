## Professional Cleaning Uniform

**Head protection**
- White or light grey standard safety hard hat, smooth plastic look
- Small cleaning company logo / broom / mop symbol on front
- Visible chin strap (black or grey)

**Safety vest**
- Neon yellow (`#FFD700`), high-visibility, reflective stripes
- White reflective stripes in an X pattern across the chest, plus one around the waist and one around the mid-back
- Worn over the work shirt; velcro or button closure visible

**Work shirt**
- Light blue (`#87CEEB`), collared, short or long sleeves (rolled up acceptable)
- Collar visible above the vest

**Work trousers**
- Dark navy blue (`#2C3E50`), professional fit, side pockets visible, full length to ankle

**Safety boots**
- Yellow (matching vest), steel-toe, ankle-height
- Dark grey/black laces, dark rubber sole, visible silver/grey metal toe cap

- Name tag: small rectangular tag on vest (optional)
